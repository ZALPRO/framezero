# Contributor covenant code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in FrameZero a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Encouraged:** demonstrating empathy and kindness; respectful, technical
disagreement; owning mistakes and learning from them; focusing on what is best
for the project and its users; crediting work honestly (including third-party
assets and measurements).

**Unacceptable:** sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual
attention; trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or
political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others' private
information without explicit permission; plagiarism of code, assets or
prose; any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues,
and other contributions that violate this covenant, and will communicate
reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning with clarity on what was wrong.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including
   a no-interaction period with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction of any kind with the community for a
   defined period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for patterns of violation, including harassment.

## Scope

This covenant applies in all community spaces (repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions) and when an individual is officially representing the
project.

## Reporting

Report abuse to the maintainers through a private channel (see
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for contact routes). All complaints are reviewed
promptly and fairly. Maintainers are bound by the same standards.
